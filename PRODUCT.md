## Platform

web

## Users

B2B buyers and procurement contacts who need corrugated or paper-based packaging manufactured to a requirement. Inferred from PROJECT.md and the approved website brief.

## Product Purpose

The website presents FIDVI Industries Private Limited as a packaging manufacturer and collects a business enquiry. Success is a company sending a packaging requirement, not browsing a catalogue.

## Positioning

FIDVI manufactures corrugated and paper-based packaging from an industrial facility in Nagzhiri Industrial Area, Ujjain. Packaging is the starting line; the brand leaves room for later manufacturing work. Confirmed lines: “Creating Tomorrow, Inspiring Forever.” and “Manufacturing Solutions. Built for Tomorrow.”

## Operating Context

Facility address: 130, Nagzhiri Industrial Area, Dewas Road, Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh, India — 456010. Phone, email and WhatsApp are unpublished. The enquiry form validates in the browser and does not send.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed products: corrugated boxes, shipper cartons, die-cut cartons, mono cartons, offset printed boxes, 2-ply paper rolls, cello tape (printed and transparent).

Confirmed process: paper reel selection, corrugation, pasting, creasing, printing/conversion, slotting/die cutting, stitching/pasting, quality inspection, bundling and packing, dispatch.

Confirmed capabilities: corrugation, pasting, creasing, slotting, stitching, die cutting, printing, customized sizes, bulk manufacturing.

Do not invent production capacity, headcount, machinery brands, certifications, clients, statistics, GSM, flute grades, or a service-area claim.

## Brand Commitments

Legal name: FIDVI Industries Private Limited. Brand: FIDVI INDUSTRIES. Voice is B2B, manufacturing-led, specific, and honest. Gold is an accent only. Do not generate new photographs. Placeholders must not be described as FIDVI factory or product images.

## Product Principles

- Manufacture first: show material, machinery, process, then the enquiry.
- Say only what FIDVI has confirmed.
- Keep the architecture and routes; refine the incumbent site.
- The manufacturing process is the signature section.
- Conversion is “send the requirement,” not a shop checkout.
